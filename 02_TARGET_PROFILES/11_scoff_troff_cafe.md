## 2. Tourist Search Behavior & Conversion Bottleneck
1. **The St Ives Tourist Reality**:
   - St Ives is Britain's premier coastal holiday paradise, attracting over 500,000 holidaymakers, beachgoers, and Tate gallery visitors every season.
   - Tourists walking off Porthmeor beach or meandering along the narrow cobbled alleys of Fore Street look up *"best breakfast in St Ives"* or *"dog friendly lunch cafe"*.
2. **The Missing Digital Menu Friction**:
   - When holidaymakers click through from Google Maps, they land on a Facebook wall where the menu is a blurry 2021 photo of a chalkboard.
   - International & UK tourists need immediate answers on their mobile phones:
     - Is it dog-friendly? (Huge selling point in Cornwall!)
     - Are there vegan, gluten-free, or oat milk options?
     - What are the daily pancake specials and breakfast serving cutoff times?
   - With dozens of cafes packed along the harbour, any hesitation causes diners to walk into the next establishment.
