## 2. Tourist Search Behavior & Conversion Bottleneck
1. **The Acadia Tourist Reality**:
   - Over 4 million tourists visit Acadia National Park annually, heavily concentrated between June and October.
   - After a 10-mile hike around Jordan Pond or Cadillac Mountain, tourists pull out their phones and search: *"best lobster bake near me"* or *"authentic Maine lobster dinner Bar Harbor"*.
2. **The Missing Website Friction**:
   - When tourists click the link on Google Maps, they hit a cluttered Facebook page requiring a login or endlessly scrolling through off-season posts.
   - Tourists cannot quickly see:
     - Tonight's market price per pound for live steamed lobster.
     - What's included in the traditional Down-East bake (clams, corn, potatoes, blueberry pie).
     - Live dinner hours and whether outdoor seating is open.
     - A 1-tap button to call or reserve a table.
   - As a result, hundreds of hungry, high-spending tourists bounce to commercial competitors in downtown Bar Harbor.

## 3. The Bespoke Prototype Solution
- **Sensory Visual Appeal**: Showcase giant steaming red Maine lobsters cracked open with drawn butter, piping hot clam chowder, and freshly baked wild blueberry pie.

  - *Downeast Feast*: 1½ lb lobster, clam chowder cup, mussels, blueberry pie slice.
  - *Rolls & Chowder*: Heaping knuckle & claw lobster roll on toasted brioche.
- **Frictionless Tourist Utility**:
  - Live Dinner Countdown (`🟢 Serving Tonight: 4:30 PM – 9:00 PM`).
  - 1-Tap Call for Group Seating `(207) 288-4597`.
  - 1-Tap Google Maps Walking/Driving directions.
- **Emotional Pride Hook**:
  - Emphasize sustainable Gulf of Maine local lobster trap sourcing, generational Downeast seaweed steaming traditions, and pride in authentic coastal Maine hospitality.
