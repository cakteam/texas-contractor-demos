## 2. Tourist Search Behavior & Conversion Bottleneck
1. **The Byron Bay Traveler Profile**:
   - Byron Bay is Australia's premier coastal sanctuary, famous for international travelers, surfers, food lovers, and creative professionals from Sydney & Melbourne.
   - Travelers wake up after morning surf or lighthouse walks wanting high-quality coffee and aesthetically plated food: *"best breakfast Byron Bay"* or *"brunch Fletcher street"*.
2. **The Social-Media Only Bottleneck**:
   - When visitors search Google Maps, they tap the website link and hit Facebook, which is disjointed and cluttered.
   - In a competitive food town like Byron where aesthetics matter enormously, lack of a clean, editorial-style mobile menu means travelers wander over to nearby spots (like Bayleaf or Combi).
   - Vacationers want to instantly see:
     - Is there an outdoor sunlit terrace?
     - What are the house-baked French pastries and signature egg skillets?
     - What single-origin coffee beans are on bar?
     - Quick table queue or reservation instructions.
