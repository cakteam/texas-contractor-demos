## 3. 客户回复应对与商业转化话术指南 (SOP)

当商家老板在 24~48 小时内回复您的邮件时，通常会分为以下三种场景：

### 场景 A：积极好奇型（“这是怎么做的？做一套要多少钱？”）
* **心理分析**：老板被动图和专属 Demo 吸引，认可解决原站 Bug 的价值，想了解商务预算。
* **推荐应对策略**：不要直接报价过低（避免变成廉价建站外包），强调**“直接把这个做好的 Demo 接入你的域名”**：
  > *"Hi [Name], glad you like the concept! Since I've already custom-built the entire layout, mobile calculator, and copy specifically for your business, we can launch it directly under your domain in less than 48 hours.*  
  > *Typically for local contractors, I offer a simple one-time setup of \$800 – \$1,200 (including domain connection, hosting setup, and testing your lead notifications), or \$150/mo including high-speed hosting and monthly updates.*  
  > *Are you free for a quick 5-minute call this Wednesday at 10 AM to confirm your prefered notification number?"*

### 场景 B：求证/戒备型（“你们是谁？为什么做这个？”）
* **心理分析**：美国老牌承包商警惕性高，担心是骗子或机器人。
* **推荐应对策略**：强调**“真人技术专家，先做后付”**的坦荡态度：
  > *"Hi [Name], totally understand the skepticism! I'm an independent web specialist based here helping home-service contractors modernize their mobile funnels. I noticed the 404/repetition bug on your live site and built the demo myself to prove value first before asking for anything.*  
  > *You don't owe me a dime to look at it. If you want to use it to capture more leads, we can talk; if not, you're welcome to have your current web person use the layout to fix your site!"*

### 场景 C：冷淡型 / 48 小时无回复（需要轻量级跟进 Follow-up）
* **推荐跟进节奏**：间隔 3 天后，直接在原邮件下方点击回复（Re:），发送简短 2 句话：
  > *"Hey [Name], just wanted to float this to the top of your inbox in case it got buried under job estimates.*  
  > *Did you have 30 seconds to check out the mobile prototype on your phone? (Link: [Demo URL])"*
